# Financial Stress Early-Warning System

## Project Overview

This project investigates whether financial and economic indicators can provide early warning signs of increasing market stress.

Instead of attempting to predict exact stock prices, the analysis focuses on identifying changes in volatility, interest rates, economic conditions, sector performance, and market behavior that may appear before periods of significant financial instability.

## Research Question

Can a combination of market and economic indicators identify elevated financial stress before a major market decline?

## Project Goals

* Collect and organize historical financial and economic data.
* Store and analyze data using SQL.
* Calculate market-risk and economic-stress indicators.
* Compare calm market periods with periods of financial instability.
* Build a simple model that classifies market-stress conditions.
* Document findings, limitations, and possible business applications.

## Quantitative Research Process

1. Define the financial research question.
2. Collect historical market and economic data.
3. Store and organize the data using SQL.
4. Clean and validate the datasets.
5. Calculate financial indicators and rolling metrics.
6. Compare indicators across calm and stressful market periods.
7. Test whether the indicators worked on unseen historical periods.
8. Evaluate the results and document the limitations.

## Research Considerations

The analysis will avoid using future information when evaluating past market periods. This helps reduce look-ahead bias and creates a more realistic research process.

The project is designed for educational and portfolio purposes and is not intended to provide investment advice.
